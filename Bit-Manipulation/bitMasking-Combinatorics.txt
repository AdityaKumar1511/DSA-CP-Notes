Use 1 and 0 for including excluding items for making combinations.

ex: [A, B, C] 
000 : []                    100 : [A]
001 : [C]                   101 : [A, C]
010 : [B]                   110 : [A, B]
011 : [B, C]                111 : [A, B, C]

combinations: 2^n (1<<n).
