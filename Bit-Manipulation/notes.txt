MASK:
Suppose we have a binary: 00101001, and we have to do something (like checking, toggling, etc.) with 3rd bit startin from 0, from right we make a mask and do some operations (like AND, XOR, etc. ) to do our task.

To do this our aim is to do it in O(1).

Mask is like 1 at that position and 0 elsewhere
in our case: 00001000.

We make it by left shifting 1. (1<<3).
____________________________________________________________________________________________________
To check i-th bit.
x & (1<<i) != 0.
if true, means not zero, specifically 2^i. means bit is 1.
if false, means zero. means bit is 1.
____________________________________________________________________________________________________
To set i-th bit.
To make i-th bit 1 regardless of what it is.
x = x | (1<<i).
____________________________________________________________________________________________________
Clear the i-th bit.
To make i-th bit 0 regardless of what it is.
x = x & ~(1<<i).
____________________________________________________________________________________________________
Toggle the i-th bit.
1 to 0, 0 to 1.
x = x ^ (1<<i).
any n ^ 0 = n.
and 1 ^ 1 = 0, 0 ^ 1 = 1.
____________________________________________________________________________________________________
Isolating the Lowest Set Bit.
00101000 -> 00001000.
x & -x.
